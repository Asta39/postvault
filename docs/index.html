Share it. It's filed.

Tap Share on an Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or X post and choose PostVault. It lands in the right collection and you go straight back to what you were watching.

Know why you saved it.

Every post gets a two to three sentence summary, the links and accounts it mentions, and space for your own note.

Tidy up in seconds.

Long-press a post to open, move or delete it, or select several at once. Make your own collections and the sorting uses those too.

It stays on your phone.

No account and no server. Posts and their pictures are stored on your device, and one file backs everything up or moves it to a new phone.

Installed in three steps.

PostVault is not on the Play Store, so Android asks once before installing it.

  1. 1Download the APKTap the download button on your Android phone.
  2. 2Open itAllow installing from this source when Android asks.
  3. 3Share a postIn Instagram tap Share, then choose PostVault.

Smart sorting needs a free key from console.groq.com, pasted into Settings. Without one, posts are sorted by keywords and hashtags.

Changelog

v1.2.0

13 November 2026

Updating from v1.1.0 or older: one-time reinstall

This release is signed with a new permanent release key, so Android will not install it over an older version.

  1. In the old app open Settings → Backup → Export and save the file.
  2. Uninstall the old app.
  3. Install v1.2.0, then Settings → Backup → Import.

Later updates will install normally.

New

  • Select several posts: long-press a post, choose Select posts, then move or delete them together. One Undo restores them all.

v1.1.0

12 November 2026

New

  • Dark mode that follows your phone's theme
  • Pictures kept on the phone, so thumbnails keep working after Instagram's links expire
  • Backup: export every post and picture to one file, and import it again
  • Your own collections: create one from the Move sheet, rename any collection
  • Notes on each post
  • Refresh a post to fetch it again and redo the AI description
  • Home: collection covers and a Recently saved row
  • Long-press a post to open, move or delete it; deletes can be undone
  • Search: tap a tag to search it, sort a collection newest or oldest first
  • More sources: TikTok, YouTube and X links, alongside Instagram
  • After sharing a post, PostVault returns you to the app you were in

Fixed

  • Confirmation messages such as "Saved to Tech" never appeared; they now show above the bottom bar
  • Much smoother scrolling on the post and collection screens
  • The "Add a post link" sheet opened behind the keyboard

v1.0.0

6 November 2026

First release of PostVault: your Instagram saves, sorted and searchable.

What's in it

  • Save posts from Instagram's Share sheet, or paste a link
  • AI sorts each post into a collection and writes a title, tags, a short description and the links it mentions
  • Collections on the home screen, a page per collection, and search everywhere or inside one collection
  • Cream liquid-glass design that scales across phone and tablet sizes
  • Everything is stored on your phone; no account, no server
